The bakery on the corner of Sanjō opens at 6:30. By 6:45 there is a small line of people
who all clearly know each other but do not speak. I bought a melon pan, a coffee, and stood
under the awning across the street while it rained.

I keep returning to this kind of quiet — the kind that exists *before* a place has decided
what it's going to be for the day. It feels like the inverse of the work I do at home,
where everything has already happened and we're piecing the story back together from logs.

Here, nothing has happened yet. The shutters are still down. I have my coffee.
